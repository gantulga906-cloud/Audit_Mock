## Form contracts and rendering findings

The source-derived contract currently contains 1,236 editable answer/procedure/table elements across all 70 forms, including 41 genuine repeated-record tables. Headings and retained fixed clauses are separate non-editable content. Families are 32 questionnaires, one calculation form, seven documents, two strategy forms, 23 programmes, four registers and one sampling form. These are renderer families, not an assertion that all professional methods are complete.

Fields have stable row-based keys, source coordinates, answer-location hints, instructions, source responsibility, original choice labels and validation-range provenance. Programmes derive assertion/result/planned-hours/actual-hours/evidence columns from their actual source header. Repeated records are grouped beneath actual numbered multi-column headers, including selected sample units, confirmations, minutes and misstatements. A record should be instantiated for an actual item, not fabricated for every blank reserve row.

Important findings:

1. **1.4.3 title.** The baseline catalogue displays a malformed title. The companion identifies the audit engagement letter at `1.4.3 А!B3`; the new contract corrects the label to `Аудитын гэрээт ажлын захидал`. Source content is unchanged.
2. **Metadata is not the answer grid.** Enriched sheets label a column `Actual possible entries / answer location`. Dropdowns in that column explain available categories, while instructions identify original answer cells. Do not present metadata columns as the native worksheet or convert every populated cell to an input.
3. **Instruction included in helper choices.** Several dropdown ranges include a leading explanatory helper cell. For example `1.1.1А!J10` refers to `$O$1:$AI$1`; O1 repeats a long instruction and categorical labels begin at P1. The proposed UI omits that duplicate instruction, retains the original range locator and records the interpretation in `source-form-findings.json`.
4. **Composite answers remain composite.** Legal name, legal form and registration number cannot be reduced to one legal-form select. Similar caution applies to period boundaries, currency facts, owner details and narrative-plus-outcome answers. Options can assist entry while required factual/narrative detail remains available.
5. **Source role is not a login grant.** A client-supplied fact can be recorded by an assigned auditor with provenance. A client/expert/custodian label in a row does not create a portal account, system role or automatic review authority.
6. **Merged strategy groups.** `2.7.1А!S12:S15` explicitly separates area anchors from continuation lines. Shared balance/materiality values must not be counted again for each child procedure/assertion row. The preserved field instructions expose this; exact multi-field grouping remains a renderer-validation obligation.
7. **Sampling is staged.** `3.3.1А!A24,K24` links approved performance materiality from 1.3. `A28,K28` links controls reliance from 2.6. A proposed sample design, actual selected units and completed tests are separate facts. An auto-filled selection date would be false evidence.
8. **Numbers and formula counts.** Many source formulas increment row numbers. The presence of 272 formulas is not evidence of 272 financial calculations. Source percentages and accounting examples need a defined selected method and rationale.
9. **Documents have fixed clauses.** Report and letter text includes controlled wording plus specific variable facts. Fixed clauses are retained separately. A generic textarea per paragraph is not a faithful document editor or an appropriate opinion decision.
10. **Archive responsibility.** Form 5.1 includes actual custodian/access events and supporting facility-control facts. This does not make R4 a custodian or administrator, and elapsed time alone does not authorize disposal.

The derived contract intentionally keeps a binding-status limitation. Exact Excel layout reproduction, all composite typed-field splits and every financial-method calculation are not established merely by extraction. The next renderer should preserve the source's structure and exercise representative questionnaire, programme, repeating register, sampling, strategy, letter and archive families using meaningful scenarios.

Source-specific QA repaired an over-broad repeating-row heuristic. A numbered questionnaire header no longer makes an unlabeled question a table: 1.1.1 r16 remains the single acceptance/continuance decision-reference answer, and r18 remains its own independence answer. All 1.1.2 questions remain individual. Materiality's read-only example bands and header-only rows are non-editable guidance. Strategy continuations retain their own area-anchor IDs instead of merging children from different balances. Unused communication layout rows are excluded. Expert-relative and business-interest tables use different real headers. Programme output columns are taken from the actual numbered source header, including the shifted row10 header in3.1.23. Sampling's selected-unit table preserves source rows37-61. Every table now has a concrete source header and requires actual record instantiation.

## Dependencies and iterative work

The application register includes 20 curated relationships spanning acceptance, planning, sampling, fieldwork, findings, reporting and archive. Each has exact worksheet or case locators and an evidence classification. Seven relationships are direct field/source references; several others are confirmed case-level handoffs with a proposed or conditional form anchor. They are not all hard gates.

Examples include acceptance/continuance/terms/independence inputs to 1.1.1; accepted scope to planning; selected materiality and controls reliance to sample design; an applicable risk response to the tailored programme; an actual selection to tests; evaluated exceptions to a classified finding; verified corrections and current thresholds to final evaluation; actual representations and applicable quality work to readiness; and an exact issued package to assembly/custody.

Mechanically finding `1.3` in a cell may find a form reference, a numbered clause, a percentage or an example. Therefore 1,148 numeric-reference candidates are preserved separately and labeled hypotheses. They must not silently become graph edges or block all work. A source reference needs semantic confirmation, applicability, current source version, target action and an actual gate policy before enforcement.

Partial drafting remains available where useful. A missing upstream decision should identify the affected field/action and next owner. Changed relied-upon content needs targeted impact assessment and necessary renewed review. It should neither silently keep affected work green nor reset unrelated work automatically.

## Professional and review boundaries

R1-R4 are capacities on assigned work. R2 may prepare, R3 may perform authorized lower work, and a separate independent-review requirement still applies where relevant. R4 ordinary quality confirmation is distinct from formal EQR applicability, eligible appointment, performed review and actual completion/notice. Neither is automatic report-issue authority.

Preparation, submission, note response, reviewer clearance, sign-off, quality confirmation, report authorization, issue and distribution are distinct actual outcomes. An administrative task close cannot perform a professional action. Production permissions must apply to records, direct links, source files, search results, totals and exports; hidden buttons only demonstrate presentation.

The current source explicitly leaves model decisions open for pre-engagement persistence, actual per-work authority, native answer/child snapshots, calculated versus selected materiality, full final-package binding, targeted reliance/renewed review, holds/retention, external service execution, proposed sample design and persisted native-workpaper representation. Open decision records preserve their proposed resolutions without presenting them as approved implementation.

## Review limits

This pass reopens the actual current registers, normalizes every current row, reads all companion worksheet contents/metadata, resolves selected validation helpers and checks all five BPMN endpoint structures. It does not claim a new full visual read of every page in the 214-page map PDF, image-based stage guides, ontology books or historical 5,216-instance corpus. A historical manifest is not a count of attachments currently supplied, and a previous review log is not a new independent read.

The BPMN maps have zero unresolved source/target endpoints in the parsed sequence flows. Unnamed gateway branches are recorded as questions, not automatically declared wrong: a default or semantically documented route can be legitimate. Whole-graph correctness, every gateway condition and visual legibility need their own review evidence.

Source standards and local-law citations are retained as source claims. This source pass does not verify current legal text, professional effective dates or Mongolian adoption. No compliance certification, functioning external integration or production access enforcement is claimed.
