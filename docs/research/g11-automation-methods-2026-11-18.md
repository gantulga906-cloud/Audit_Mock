## Declared calculation boundaries

Money is calculated in integer minor units with guarded BigInt products/totals and explicit half-away-from-zero rounding. Physical quantities in these profiles are declared integer units; incompatible units and unsupported fractional profiles remain unresolved. Invalid, missing, ambiguous, out-of-scope or overflowing inputs cannot become zero or a favorable complete total. Quarantined rows retain raw identity/value/reasons and no complete total is calculated from a silently reduced population.

Calendar-month allocation requires exact full-month boundaries; inclusive-day allocation is separately selected. Depreciation requires a full calendar reporting year, explicit actual eligible day/month/full-year convention, supplied life/residual/opening accumulated amounts and a nonnegative remaining base. Charges are capped to that remaining base. Excess prior accumulated charge is not automatically reversed. Periodic weighted-average costing reconciles quantities and both recorded COS/closing value, with explicitly rounded total issue cost and residual closing cost; other costing methods remain outside this profile.

Identity normalization is exact or explicitly trim/uppercase only; identity-significant punctuation is preserved. Shared attributes remain candidates. One source transaction contributes exposure once across shared-field clusters; a verified reciprocal transfer is one event. Exact document-line joins preserve one-to-many partial quantities and payments without multiplying header amounts. Missing/ambiguous links are not inferred. Related-party totals use declared parties only; unconfirmed shared identifiers stay separate.

Estimate rates come from the selected fictional/actual approved pack; source exposure keys are disjoint within the estimate. This does not validate a model or make an ECL/impairment decision. Consolidation retains component import, mapping, supplied elimination and reciprocal residuals separately; no elimination journal or currency translation is generated. Liquidity indicators require explicit restrictions/availability, denominator and forecast horizon. Unknown facilities remain unknown; no breach within a supplied forecast means beyond that forecast, not perpetual viability.
