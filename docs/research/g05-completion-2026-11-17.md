# G05: findings, completion, consultation and independent quality

17 November 2026. Engineering implementation checkpoint following the G05 handbook and PILOT. The source handbook remains the requirements reference; this guide explains the implemented core and its retained boundaries.

## Working order

1. Open an accepted, planned engagement with actual reviewed fieldwork. The partner explicitly enables the G05 completion profile. Existing engagements retain their earlier behavior until this choice; enabling does not adopt old approvals as new G05 decisions.
2. Record the actual matter, source work, unit/reference, nature, condition, criterion, cause, consequence and accountable next work. Incomplete drafts retain unknowns. Submission requires actual support; a blank financial amount cannot become zero. Restricted fraud/noncompliance circumstances retain their separate role scope.
3. Independently evaluate the matter. A financial consequence links the canonical finding instead of creating a second copy of the same issue. A control or evidence consequence remains visible without inventing a monetary misstatement. An unresolved consequence requires a limitation and actual next work.
4. Record management's actual response separately from an independently verified correction. A 100 finding, agreement of 100 and actual posting of 60 leave 40 outstanding. Reclassification checks each signed line against actual compatible final population rows and prevents reuse of those rows by another correction. A balanced journal alone is insufficient. Disclosure correction requires actual changed final statements/notes and their reviewed management package; zero monetary value is insufficient evidence of correction.
5. Complete eight distinct assessments: statements, selected disclosures, representations, communication, other information, legal matters, going concern and subsequent events. Use the actual final period, statement version, source work and accepted original documents. An applicable missing disclosure retains an explicit gap. Supported N/A needs a reason. Expected-later information is recorded as expected later, with an actual agreement and owner, rather than as a performed read. No generic assessment creates audit assurance over other information.
6. Use current independently reviewed final analytics and explicit individual, qualitative, prior-period and reassessment judgments for final evaluation, including engagements with no monetary findings. Known incompatible currencies are rejected; no implicit conversion or netting is provided. G04 incremental projection boundaries remain intact.
7. Resolve consultations through actual facts, permitted advice, performed application, supporting originals and resolution authority. Advice alone does not resolve an issue. A professional prohibition cannot be waived by copying it into an application narrative. Independent review and exact source currentness remain required.
8. Independently clear anchored fieldwork and quality concerns against the exact response/work version. A team response is distinct from independent clearance, and clearance is distinct from the final stand-back decision.
9. Record ordinary assigned R4 final confirmation separately from formal EQR applicability, eligibility and performance. Ordinary confirmation receives only selected work and related actual material. Formal EQR retains its distinct full-package route. Eligibility evidence covers competence, capability, authority, objectivity and the cooling-off position; assignment alone is insufficient. Actual performed scope, judgments, conclusion and completion notice are recorded. New concerns invalidate the earlier confirmation even when the paper has not changed; clearing the concern requires a fresh final confirmation. An unrelated statement change does not reopen an ordinary selected-paper confirmation without an impact basis.
10. Hand the current completion position to the existing report-authority controls. G05 does not issue a report. G06 remains the next implementation guide.

## Exact versions and retained history

The profile uses the existing SQLite snapshot, original evidence and canonical finding/correction registers. Records retain draft/submission/review versions and events. Their basis captures relevant final statements, report date, management package, reviewed source work, original evidence version/hash and linked completion documents. Source correction invalidates affected reliance while preserving earlier originals, approvals and authorship. It does not silently repin an old decision to a new document.

The shared outstanding-work queue shows current limitations, unreviewed or stale cases, actual workpaper notes, missing final evaluation and applicable quality work with the accountable next owner. Generic sign-off cannot convert unknown impact into current reliance.

## Conditional service

Recommendations and implementation follow-up require actual separate authority and a current mandate window. A management completion claim remains distinct from independent performed verification. An expired mandate cannot authorize a new follow-up. Extension decisions retain an actual new due date and reason. These service records do not reopen the statutory audit package merely because an unrelated management action changes. They also do not rewrite the original finding or opinion.

This is a bounded pre-archive route. A separately authorized service workspace after statutory archive, complete failed-effectiveness-retest alternatives, service-specific source-finding reconciliation and all A29/A30 branches remain explicit development/acceptance work. Existing sealed-audit write protections remain in force.

## Verification and compatibility

Seven dedicated scenarios exercise the connected 100/60/40 cycle, distinct final documents, reclassification/disclosure effects, ordinary R4 versus formal EQR, conditional authority, applied consultation, and incomplete/restricted matters. Existing regression tests retain earlier acceptance, planning, data, sampling, fieldwork, technical, report, custody and later-fact behavior. Actual browser checks run against disposable fixture copies. Public checkpoint evidence records the exact revision, expected/actual observations and executed scope.

The original 25 development packages, 128 tasks and 2,247 registered source obligations remain preserved. Full source-case acceptance is distinct from these engineering fixtures. Separate observations are not combined into fabricated complete end-to-end lifecycles. Manual communication receipts do not imply an external message was sent.

## Retained work

- Full A19–A24 and A29/A30 branch packs and independent source acceptance, including complete ordinary/formal quality alternatives and conditional-service failure/extension cases.
- Firm-specific eligibility, methodology, adoption and actual professional decisions. Prototype role scope is not production identity or document-level access control.
- The selected disclosure checklist is an actual explicit selection, not a universal jurisdictional checklist. No automatic legal, fraud, going-concern or opinion conclusion is generated.
- No new FX aggregation or incompatible-currency evaluation. Remaining G10 complex methods, AUTO-044/045 and area-specific acceptance are not closed by generic completion forms.
- Remaining G08 native form/export adoption, G09 release/acceptance, G10/G11 methods and source contracts remain in their original backlog. Existing later-fact work is retained under G07.
