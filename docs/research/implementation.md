Cross-industry queue/inspector, shared schedule, bounded interface, record-context, revision-review, agenda and attention patterns supplement them. The16AUD-PAT and20EXT-PAT cards retain evidence limits. The design spec selects eight audit/four external patterns and supplies original text wireframes, not images of untested private products.

A schedule edit does not alter content revision or actual review. SubmittedBy remains separate from the latest owner, preventing reassignment from manufacturing independence.
