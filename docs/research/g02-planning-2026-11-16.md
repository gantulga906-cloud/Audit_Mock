# G02: staffing, planning, risk, controls and materiality

16 November 2026. Engineering implementation of the published G02 handbook. This checkpoint extends the shared pilot; source acceptance and professional applicability remain separate decisions.

## Result and compatibility

An accepted engagement can now retain its planning scope and methodology edition, actual understanding and discussion, individually evaluated controls, multiple risk responses, an independently approved strategy, a usable version-specific handoff and actual named-person effort. Existing staffing, materiality, sampling, automation, native workpapers, review, reporting, quality and archive paths remain in place.

New standard extended enquiry conversions enable G02 alongside G01. Earlier engagements retain their earlier behavior until the partner explicitly enables G02 from Planning with a reason. Enabling it preserves earlier records and signatures and exposes the current planning requirements. It does not manufacture approvals. Earlier direct-created and basic synthetic profiles retain their legacy contracts.

No new planning database or parallel workpaper store was introduced. Records use the existing scoped, versioned register, independent review, immutable history, transaction and command-revision system. Actual effort uses an append-only engagement journal included in the report authorization stamp and retained authorization snapshot. The shared source registers, original backlog and independent acceptance register are unchanged.

## Working order

1. Complete current G01 acceptance, written terms and separate activation. Confirm the approved named roster, credentials, appointment, independence and scoped access; the three preview roles remain distinct capacities, not a claim that every firm needs a universal four-person team.
2. In **Planning → Audit registers**, approve **M05-F02**, the current scope, authority reference, method edition, accepted source, actual firm assertion vocabulary and relevant group/component scope. No invented default risk scale or assertion codes are supplied.
3. Record **M06-F02** understanding, including systems/interfaces, service organizations, transactions/policies/changes and unanswered facts with owners and effects. Record actual fraud inquiries in **M06-F04**, investigation candidates in **M06-F06**, and **M06-F09** risks. An analytical variance is not promoted to a fraud finding. An overall financial-statement risk has no fictitious account, assertion or procedure. Its staffing/supervision response belongs in **M06-F11**.
4. Use **M06-F03** for relevant controls. Record the objective, risk, owner, frequency, system, walkthrough, design, implementation and operating tests separately. Supported reliance needs adequate design, observed implementation and effective operating tests, with actual population, period, sample rationale, performance evidence, deviation assessment and conclusion. Partial-period/prior-period support needs its own explanation and accepted evidence. Failed/deficient controls require a recorded follow-up and cannot supply supported reliance.
5. Keep the existing independently reviewed **1.3** materiality decision. Candidates, selected thresholds, genuine zero, specific amounts and revisions retain their established validation. **2.6** remains available as the native controls workpaper and is still pinned for a reliance programme. A substantive programme gains no blanket dependency on 2.6.
6. In **M07-F01**, select the approved methodology rule, actual source/published template, primary risk/procedure, approach and nature/timing/extent. The additional response editor supports several procedures for a risk and several risks for a procedure. A risk can name additional actual programme IDs. Every edge pins its risk and optional control version and exact procedure; its scope rationale records the auditor's judgment. It does not decide whether that judgment is professionally sufficient. Legacy single-risk/procedure decisions remain valid in their earlier profiles.
7. **M06-F05** distinguishes auditor's expert, management's expert, internal audit function, direct assistance, component auditor and opening balances. Delivered work requires auditor suitability and method/data/results evaluation. Opening limitations require comparative/policy and follow-up treatment. Direct assistance needs explicit source-backed permission; component work needs relevant approved group scope. An unresolved/inadequate contribution remains a completion gap; an explicit limitation must reach the later evidence-sufficiency evaluation. No contributor gains note-clearance, opinion or general application access through this record.
8. Approve **M06-F13** actual discussion and decisions. An invitation stays an invitation. Approve **M05-F04** dates and conflict treatment; an asserted statutory deadline needs accepted authority evidence. An open conflict cannot enter an approved strategy. An independently approved scheduling-risk acceptance is not a waiver of audit or EQR requirements. Approve **M05-F05**, a dated planned-hour baseline with its scope and change rationale.
9. Independently approve **M06-F14** for each adopted programme. It pins the scope profile, actual held discussion, programme, materiality through the programme, applicable risks/responses, dates, budget and suitable team assignments. An uncovered or unreviewed relevant risk prevents approval. The impact decision explains changed work and supported unaffected work. A new relevant risk, changed linked source or changed assignment reopens the affected baseline.
10. Approve **M05-F08** with the current strategy, actual programme/work versions, actual next owner, open work and restrictions. A restricted handoff stays open. A ready handoff requires a current assigned recipient with engagement access. Revoking that recipient's access reopens the handoff; granting it back does not resurrect the earlier approval. The affected handoff needs renewed independent review.
11. Execute and independently review the planned procedures in the existing workpaper workflow. Every selected response procedure needs actual execution and an allowed outcome. Earlier answers and signatures remain historical when a plan changes. Required work, source evidence and later review/reporting gates are not deleted by a planning choice.

## Actual effort and capacity

The Planning desk records the actor's actual assigned work, timezone-qualified start/end, purpose and calculated minutes. A single record covers at most 24 hours, must be in the past and must fall within that actor's current named assignment. The system rejects overlaps for the same person across engagements with a generic message. It accepts effort above estimates or capacity: actual effort is never clipped to make a budget feasible.

Corrections require a reason and append a replacement; they do not rewrite the original entry. A person cannot correct another person's effort or revive an already superseded entry. Historical identities and assignment versions remain. Budget changes do not mutate effort. The engagement and firm views label planned and actual intervals separately; a preparer sees their own effort. The firm portfolio shows only permitted engagement contributions and does not represent utilization as professional audit quality. The roster's capacity denominator remains the declared full availability window; it is not inferred weekly availability.

## Programme adoption loss review

Dedicated programme adoption now follows the same loss-review contract as direct form adoption. The submitted decision exposes incompatible answers, detached evidence and the current migration digest. Approval with missing, incorrect or stale acknowledgement fails transactionally. The independent reviewer must inspect and explicitly acknowledge the current losses. The approved decision retains actor/time/digest, and the workpaper retains the migration receipt, earlier answers, links and old template history. A later template publication never silently migrates an adopted programme.

## Verification and future work

The checkpoint directory records executed regression/build results, G00 contract checks, source inventories, G02 criterion evidence, connected expected/actual planning observations, actual browser verification and byte manifests. The old 34-register reference journey still explicitly checks its original set; the eight additional G02 register types are exercised separately. Source-based verification links are bounded to their real fixtures; a partial source journey is not counted as a complete A-series cycle.

All 25 original packages and 128 tasks, 2,247 registered source obligations, 70 forms, 22 variants, 58 minimum automations, 58 pending extensions, 20 dependencies and 1,012 BPMN rows remain. This checkpoint does not silently accept, remove, narrow or defer any of them. G03 and later implementation continues in handbook order. Existing E2E/reporting/quality/custody requirements still govern the final system.

The current profile uses preset role accounts mapped to named roster people, manual accepted source evidence and human professional judgments. Firm-approved method/ratings, actual local legal adoption, production identity integrations, conditional specialist portals and future AI allocation/grading are not invented by this implementation. No independent source acceptance was entered. No live-preview database migration, main merge or deployment was performed.

Professional distinctions were checked against the official [IAASB 2025 Handbook, Volume 1](https://ifacweb.blob.core.windows.net/publicfiles/2025-09/IAASB-2025-Handbook-Volume-1.pdf): ISA 315 A177 and ISA 330 A26 distinguish inquiry from sufficient controls evidence; ISA 610 paragraphs 26–35 makes direct assistance conditional and separately supervised; ISA 620 paragraphs 9–13 addresses expert suitability and evaluation. These references inform the record structure. The actual applicable edition and local permission remain engagement-specific, source-backed judgments.
