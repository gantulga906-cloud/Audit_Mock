## Sampling and FG-01

The new opt-in method is `hmac-sha256-rejection-fy-v1`: a retained 256-bit randomly generated entropy value, displayed integer seed and counter produce HMAC-SHA256 words; unbiased bounded rejection feeds a partial Fisher–Yates draw without replacement. Ordered eligible IDs/hash, source version/hash, source-row references, exact method, entropy, seed and size are retained for reproduction. This removes modulo bias under the pseudorandom-word model; it is not a confidence or assurance certification. Old `deterministic-row-selection-v1` selections remain unchanged and clearly identified.

The auditor separately identifies specific/full-tested, excluded and residual groups with actual reasons. IDs cannot overlap; count and signed book amounts reconcile to the accepted mapped source. Selection follows independent approval. All specifically selected items and drawn residual units need actual testing. There is no automatic replacement of unavailable support. A suitable actual alternative can be recorded; unexplained inability prevents usable projection. The approved size remains an auditor decision, at most 500 total tested units, within the actual eligible population.

Each new-method test retains book amount, audited amount, signed difference (`book − audited`) and separately derived absolute exposure. The native source's signed-difference requirement is preserved without changing historical nonnegative findings. A specifically necessary **per-design partner decision**, supported by current accepted methodology evidence, must approve `book-minus-audited-gross-incremental-v1` before projection. Its boundaries are one currency, positive single-sign residual book units, same-direction errors, separate gross exposure and incremental projection. The synthetic fixture approval is not a firm-wide resolution of HB-D02 or professional acceptance of the method.

For residual book 1000, sample book 200 and signed error 10, inclusive projected error is 50. The retained factual finding is 10 and the additional projected finding is 40, totaling 50. A separate fully tested specific error 10 adds once, giving 60. Negative same-direction differences retain their direction; exposure is separate. Mixed-direction errors, nonpositive book units/denominators, missing convention approval, controls objectives and untested/currentness failures return explicit not-evaluable reasons, never an invented zero or confidence interval. Integer-cent ratio projection rounds half away from zero with exact integer products.

Expansion creates a new independently approved design, keeps the prior ordered selection as a prefix, draws additional unselected residual units and carries the actual earlier tests with their origin version. Previous selections, tests and review history remain retrievable. Findings have stable source/unit identity; repeated tests and exact command retries do not duplicate them. A finding whose separate resolution has started cannot be erased or silently replaced by a later passing test. A different source population creates a new reliance basis; overlapping old/new findings still require accountable G05 adjudication, not automatic deletion or inferred netting.

## Actual procedures and technical judgments

- Detail tests retain both inspected values and the auditor's disposition. The same issue found by another procedure can explicitly link its existing finding at the same amount; a changed monetary consequence cannot silently reuse that amount.
- A real control-instance deviation makes supported reliance noncurrent while retaining distinct design, implementation and operating assessments. The relevant G02 response must be reconsidered. Walkthroughs do not imply period-long operation.
- Substantive analytics require suitability, reliable-data basis, expectation, precision and threshold **before** actual comparison. A difference above that threshold requires separate current corroborating evidence and actual investigation, beyond a plausible explanation. G03 comparative analytics alone do not become substantive assurance.
- Inventory records distinguish actual attendance/instructions, both-direction tests, location/item/quantity/unit, final-record reconciliation, intervening movement and unobserved scope. Existing structured inventory calculations remain separate supporting methods; this record does not fabricate physical attendance or automate unsupported costing/NRV methods.
- Estimate, going-concern and component evaluations retain their actual source and statement version. Going concern separates basis, uncertainty, disclosures and evidence limitation. Conditional components require the actual approved G02 contributor evaluation; a component report does not replace group-auditor evaluation. Unsupported complex calculations remain manual/specialist work with explicit limitations.
- Subsequent-event work retains the actual sources and performed-through date. A later report date invalidates the earlier conclusion; the date alone cannot extend work that was not performed.

## Whole-journal investigation

The new `journal-whole-multiset-v2` method adds exact sorted whole-journal fingerprints that preserve line multiplicities. In the independent example, J1/J2 each A+10,B−10 match; J3 A+10,B−5,B−5 does not. Net zero alone is not duplication. Duplicate, manual, amount, period-end and optional declared round-unit triggers share **one case per journal**; gross debit/credit are counted once, not once per trigger.

The run pins actual complete-population/nonordinary-adjustment coverage evidence and selected parameters. Selected journals with excluded or missing retained lines are rejected. Actual purpose, authority, accounting and supporting evidence are investigated before independent review. A machine trigger is not fraud or a concluded misstatement. User rarity, near-duplicate text, automatic reversals and unsupported missing user/time fields are not invented. Earlier `journal-posting-filters-v1` history remains valid under its own profile.
