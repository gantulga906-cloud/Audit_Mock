## Use and common execution contract

These 22 cards preserve separate source procedure/acceptance obligations. They specialize G04; they do not grant complete automation credit to every area. Use current approved scope/risk/programme, accepted exact sources, declared method/edition, actual auditor work, independent review and changed-source preservation from G00/G03/G04. Examples below are independent synthetic arithmetic or decision cases, not application executions or approved policies. Each card needs normal, exception, missing-input, unauthorized/self-review and changed-source scenarios. G05 receives at least one resulting finding before a variant can be accepted as connected.

## P09-UC02-V01 - Intangible assets

Source: F00788 / 3.1.1А!C10:C22; Audit program — intangible assets. Scope basis: M07-F07; M07-F11; M07-F12; related approved programme. Source form procedures are tailored, not all mandatory.

Trigger: A newly recorded software/license cost needs support and the year's amortization is being tested.

Required inputs: Intangible register and movement list; license/development contracts; costs; approved useful-life/method basis.

Preparer: Inspect rights and selected additions/disposals, distinguish the nature of expenditure and recalculate the supported amortization.

Reviewer: Challenge rights, the recognition/capitalization basis, useful life and unsupported classifications; arithmetic is only part of the work.

Professional boundary: No automatic recognition decision from a cost label or a successful amortization calculation.

Current execution profile: No dedicated area engine is registered for this form in areaProfiles; native work/manual evidence and shared sampling/technical records may support part of the work. Do not call this absence of the entire business workflow.

Independent synthetic expectation: 120 cost / 3 years = 40 annual charge under a selected straight-line full-year profile. Rights and capitalization evidence are a separate prerequisite.

Exception/missing/unsupported: Unsupported right/recognition basis prevents an automated asset conclusion even if arithmetic is correct.

Version/change behavior: pin actual programme, population, source files, parameters/edition and work/assignment versions. Changed relied-upon evidence creates a new run or renewed affected review; keep old selection, results, support and sign-off. Wrong scope and self-review are denied. Same intended retry cannot duplicate the case/finding.

Acceptance/build: P09-UC02-V01-NORMAL proves the stated outcome; -EXCEPTION carries a supported matter into G05; -MISSING prevents a false zero/pass; -UNAUTH checks actual role/scope; -CHANGE replaces a relied-upon source and proves selective reopening/restart. Current variant-specific independent acceptance is open. R16.1/R16.6 apply to every card, with R16.2 commercial, R16.3 inventory/assets, R16.4 statutory/finance and R16.5 technical contributions as applicable.
