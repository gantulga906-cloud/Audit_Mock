## Purpose and real completion boundary

Carry observed matters into explained findings, actual client responses, independently verified corrections and a current final evaluation. Complete period/reporting-date matters, statements/disclosures, representations, governance communication, consultations and applicable independent quality work. The responsible auditor forms conclusions; software records support, checks exact current dependencies and routes outstanding work.

Current code supports finding classification, management response, independent posting verification, exact-version evaluation, technical input changes, versioned consultation, review-note history and separate quality review. A25 is connected. A19-A24/A30 and broader technical/judgment alternatives still require implementation and proof; they are not empty nor complete. G06 owns authorization/issue after these inputs; G07 owns custody and later facts.

## Records and ownership

| Record | Authority and consumer |
| --- | --- |
| Finding and fact | Stable reference/source work, actual classification/effect/amount, evidence ID/version/hash and current work revision. Avoid duplicate event exposure. |
| Correction response/posting | Management decision/response evidence distinct from actual ledger posting; independent verifier binds current approved population and amount. |
| Evaluation | Statement version, reviewed thresholds and current outstanding finding/posting inputs, reviewer preparation and independent partner approval. |
| Technical matter/consultation | Affected workpaper/version, issue, advice/support, accountable decision and prohibition/difference status. Advice is not automatically a cleared conclusion. |
| Completion/representation/communication | Actual final statements/notes, responsible approval, dates/scope/signatories, discussion/distribution support and unresolved matters. |
| Quality policy and review | Required/not-required/unresolved applicability; eligible/ineligible/unresolved reviewer; actual scoped exact-draft review and outstanding concerns. |

## Finding through correction

Current completion-finding-record supports factual/projected/qualitative classifications on a reviewed Stage 3 workpaper, a unique reference and accepted original support. It accepts nonnegative monetary amounts; qualitative amount is zero. A projected label does not compute a projection. The engine currently compares gross absolute outstanding exposure; HB-D02 must define any signed accounting/netting/projection extension without rewriting old findings.

Synthetic recorded example: management agrees 100, independent ledger evidence verifies 60, and 40 stays outstanding. Neither agreement nor a reported posting attempt removes 100 automatically. Changing the approved population or response evidence invalidates the prior posting basis. Current evaluation pins statements/materiality/findings and requires a separate partner approval. Qualitative significance is retained even where amount is zero or below monetary thresholds.

## Technical work and completion judgments

Related-party ownership evidence, estimates/assumptions, component/specialist inputs and legal/contingency/going-concern/subsequent-event matters need actual auditor assessment, alternative or contradictory support and documented effect on response/conclusion/report. A model output is not an adequate conclusion by itself. Management experts, auditor experts, internal auditors and component auditors have different purposes/responsibility; receiving their report does not transfer the auditor's responsibility.

Final statements and notes require the actual current version and approval. Written representations have their own actual authorship/date/scope and do not replace other evidence. Governance communication records whom/when/what/outcome; creating a draft is not delivery. Other information A30 has its own applicability, version/read work, apparent inconsistency/investigation and response; it must not disappear inside a generic completion checkbox. Conditional recommendation follow-up P13/A29 needs an agreed mandate and owner; the original eight conditional features remain visible and inactive until that decision.

## Review, consultation and quality transitions

Save, submit, return, respond, clear and review remain distinct. An anchored review note identifies exact work/response versions, author, reason and date. A preparer response cannot clear the reviewer's concern. Significant differences/prohibitions require the actual responsible resolution and may block affected completion.

Current consultation ties the issue/advice to a reviewed workpaper and accepted advice evidence. A change to that work/evidence makes the resolution noncurrent while preserving the prior signed decision. Formal EQR is selected only when actually required by applicable policy/law; ordinary R4 confirmation is separate. Required review needs an eligible independent account. Unassessed/ineligible candidates cannot clear the draft; a not-required decision creates no fictional review. Quality review is tied to exact current draft basis and unresolved concerns, on/before the report date in the supported contract. Source 'strictly before' wording remains a documented conflict; any earlier firm deadline needs approval.

## Failures and changed input

Wrong work scope, unreviewed fieldwork, duplicate finding reference, unaccepted evidence, negative unsupported amount, self-review and stale evaluation are rejected. Uncertain management posting remains unverified. Missing/contradictory evidence must retain the open issue/owner and possible scope limitation. New source facts reopen linked work/consultation/evaluation and the exact downstream report/quality basis; unrelated sign-offs stay intact. Issued bytes are never edited; post-issue facts go to G07's controlled process.
