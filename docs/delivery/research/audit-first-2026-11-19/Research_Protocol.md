# Mandatory research-to-implementation protocol

**Methodology:** requirements engineering with standards traceability, professional methodology governance and independent numerical validation. The object is an implementable, reviewable audit capability. Research is proportionate to the source promise and risk; it must resolve actual decisions, not accumulate undirected reading.

## 1. Binding research mandate

Every corresponding domain, form, procedure, automation, calculation and decision table must consider applicable **International Standards on Auditing (ISA)**, **International Standards on Quality Management (ISQM 1/2)** and **IESBA ethics/independence**, together with applicable **IFRS Accounting Standards/IAS/IFRIC/SIC** or the separately selected **IFRS for SMEs** framework. Public-sector/specialized frameworks are applicability decisions, not automatically promised new engines. Local law/adoption/regulated-sector requirements and approved firm policy are separate evidence layers.

An ISA tells the auditor what work/responsibility is required; an accounting standard governs relevant recognition/measurement/presentation/disclosure; firm policy chooses permitted methodology parameters. Neither a source workbook nor a competitor feature establishes legal/professional authority. Standards generally do not prescribe every software field, percentage, sampling formula, depreciation life, risk score or technical access-control design. Identify chosen product controls as design decisions where appropriate.

## 2. Research sequence for every task

1. **Pin scope and baseline:** original requirement/case/form/AUTO/variant/DEP/BPMN IDs; source bytes/edition/coordinates; existing G-guide/checkpoint and actual runtime command. State AS-IS evidence and unknowns.
2. **Extract source meaning:** actual work, actor, decision, evidence, calculation, exception, conclusion, review and downstream effect. Read visible page/figure content. Classify cosmetic numbering versus operative formula/validation. Flag encoding-corrupt locators for original-source verification.
3. **Retrieve authority:** search official issuer/regulator sources first, then fetch full applicable requirements and related application material, definitions, appendices, effective/transition clauses and relevant interpretations. Record exact paragraphs, edition and retrieval date. Public synopses seed research only; unread/paywalled text remains a gap.
4. **Establish applicability:** framework, entity type, jurisdiction, period start/end, report date, local adoption instrument, early adoption and firm policy. Separate final/issued-not-effective/proposal/superseded. More demanding firm policy is identified as policy, not attributed to law.
5. **Compare and challenge:** reconcile workbook, guidebook, international rule, local adoption and current software. Seek an exception/counterexample that could change the answer. Explain conflicts; do not average or silently select convenient sources.
6. **Design the contract:** fields/types/grain/units, actual action, result, human judgment, evidence, review, next work, permission, source-change/staleness and export. State exact supported/unsupported boundary. Prefer extending the owning records/commands.
7. **Validate method and scenarios:** independently expected numerical examples or approved professional decision tables; meaningful adverse, missing, unsupported, denied, changed-source, historical and retry/concurrent cases. Quantitative outputs never imply autonomous audit judgment.
8. **Prepare reviewable decision and ticket:** evidence, alternatives, proposed interpretation/policy, assumptions and impact. Authorized professional decides unresolved methodology/law/scope; engineer sizes specific delta; QA and practitioner acceptance remain separate.

## 3. Source authority and conflict treatment

| Evidence layer | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Current/historical applicable local primary law and adoption instruments | Verify consolidated text and amendments; exact article/date and competent interpretation. An international standard cannot waive a local restriction. |
| Original international standard/pronouncement | Exact edition and clauses; distinguish requirements from application guidance and future proposals. International effectiveness does not itself prove local adoption. |
| Applicable firm/client policy elections | Authoritative approval and version; distinguish allowed choice from mandatory rule and from implementation convention. |
| User-supplied source form/guide/BPMN | Product/source requirement with exact ID/locator; reconcile professional conflict before adopting operative logic. Preserve original. |
| Official implementation guidance / national adoption | Useful interpretation/comparison with status/limitations; not a substitute for original applicable text. Exclude foreign additions before relying on equivalence. |
| Original statistical/numerical research | Needed when a chosen algorithm has assumptions beyond an audit standard; independently reproduce selected results. Academic evidence does not override professional applicability. |
| Vendor product pages / peers | Optional workflow/usability evidence only; no compliance or mathematical authority. |

If primary authority is inaccessible, obtain an authorized official/firm copy or keep the affected conclusion pending. SRC-05/06 currently have this limitation. Do not infer a Mongolian prohibition or tax/retention rule from a search snippet or IFAC summary alone.

## 4. Required traceability record

For each obligation record original IDs → source edition/hash/page/cell → standard/paragraph and adoption → interpreted requirement/policy/judgment → domain/task/method → UI/command/persisted fields → independent expected case → actual executed result → export/handoff → source/professional/operational acceptance. Record separate states for each link. Link coverage is not proof of correctness.

Required full-standard matrix fields: issuer/title; edition/version; paragraph/article; exact topic/requirement paraphrase; mandatory versus guidance; international date/rule; local adoption evidence/date; framework/entity/period applicability; affected original IDs; conflict; decision owner/reviewer; selected rule and evidence; future review trigger. Templates are supplied. No paragraph numbers or signatures are prefilled from memory.

## 5. Gate model and evidence states

| Gate | Required evidence | May proceed afterwards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G-R0 Scoped | Original IDs/source availability; owning task and actual AS-IS; candidate standards and specific research questions | Targeted reading, source comparison and safe prototypes under explicit assumptions |
| G-R1 Research resolved | Full applicable source/standard evidence, authority/applicability, contradiction check and unresolved decisions bounded | Proposed design/method and independent example preparation |
| G-R2 Method/design approved | Authorized policy/interpretation, typed contract, independent expected cases, exact unsupported boundary, reviewer and impact | Specific implementation ticket READY; named owner/size/dependencies can be set |
| G-E Engineering verified | Persisted implementation/output matches independent cases; roles/change/retry/history/export evidence on pinned build | Practitioner source/usability acceptance |
| G-A Professionally accepted | Attributable independent auditor accepts actual required source execution and method/output on exact build/editions | Source acceptance for that bounded capability |
| G-O Operationally accepted | Approved deployment workload/access/recovery/provider obligations proven by actual owners | Release only when all applicable required gates complete |

All 60 research tasks, 36 M packs, 22 area rows, 58 AUTO rows and 20 DEP rows in this new pack start **PLANNED**. Retrieved source-page statuses describe only the preliminary research used to design this plan. Existing G01–G11 engineering evidence and historical source statuses remain intact. No new professional acceptance or releaseReady claim is created.

Manual professional judgment can be a complete required software-supported path when the original requirement calls for judgment. It must still capture actor/evidence/rationale/review. A manual value field cannot fulfill an original promise to automatically calculate, reconcile or evaluate a supported numerical method without explicit authorized scope disposition.

## 6. Independence and validation quality

Method author, numerical checker, implementing developer, QA and professional acceptance are separate capacities. Assign actual people; do not fabricate names or signatures. Numerical independence means expected values do not call or copy the production algorithm. Tiny transparent hand checks plus a different workbook/script/reference are preferred where appropriate. Statistical methods need documented derivation/reference assumptions. Non-numeric decisions need professional case expectations, not meaningless numeric tests.

Round monetary values at the specified level using declared decimal convention, currency scale and tolerance; report both unrounded basis and rounded results where necessary. Distinguish null/unknown/zero/not-applicable, signed error/gross exposure, transaction/functional/presentation currency, quantity/unit cost and report/transaction/issue/discovery dates. Test relevant interactions, not every irrelevant edge case for every rule.

## 7. Change, scheduling and resource control

A new source/standard/adoption/method/input/policy version triggers impact analysis and a new accepted version where required. Retain historical work and prior approvals; invalidate only actually dependent current results. Requirements with unresolved affected mandatory blockers remain open. Unrelated tasks can continue.

Use the dependency queue in the index. Research enough of one complete vertical slice to make it READY, then implement/review that slice while other required source research continues. Do not require all international standards to be fully researched before any bounded work. No duration, budget, staffing count or completion percentage is asserted. Size tickets after source/method triage; owner assignments and acceptance capacity are actual project decisions.
