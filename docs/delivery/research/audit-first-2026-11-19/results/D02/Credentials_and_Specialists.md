# Credential evidence and contributor classification — R-D02-03/04

**Specified:** 19 November 2026. Credential checking is evidence management plus a human suitability decision. An API, document image, credential title or attendance certificate cannot automatically establish competence or statutory audit authority.

## Credential and suitability records

| Field | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| person_id / issuer / credential_type / credential_id | Durable person, actual issuing body and exact identifier. Duplicate issuer/type/ID is flagged for investigation; do not merge people automatically. Degree, CPA, CFA, specialist licence and experience are distinct evidence categories. |
| issued_on / valid_from / validity_kind / valid_to | Date or explicitly unknown; validity_kind = dated / no-expiry / unknown. No-expiry needs supporting basis; blank expiry is not lifetime authority. Known dated authority must cover the action/appointment interval. A degree need not have an invented expiry. |
| jurisdiction / permitted_scope / restrictions / standing | Scope and current standing of a credential/authorization, including suspension/revocation and evidence date. Credential possession, membership and permission to practise are separate. |
| evidence_refs[] | Exact file/version or issuer response/public-register record, source URL/reference, inspected date and evaluator. Retain original bytes and provenance. Upload never proves authenticity. |
| verification_status | self-reported / file-reviewed / externally-verified. File-reviewed means evaluator inspected the evidence; externally-verified requires an actual issuer/register/authorized third-party response, with method/date/result. Manual issuer lookup is sufficient; no live integration promised. |
| competence_evidence[] | Actual sector/work experience, skills, relevant training, prior-work assessment and limits. No numeric competence or audit-quality score. |
| suitability | engagement/work scope, person, resource/competence/ethics/authority evidence versions, evaluator, date, conclusion suitable / restricted / unsuitable / pending, restrictions, review trigger, reviewer where required |

Expire a dated permission at the end of its declared valid-to local day, converted to an explicit instant. A later expiry/revocation blocks relevant future reserved acts and prompts reassignment. Past valid work remains historical; if later information shows prior ineligibility, open an impact assessment. Replacing an attachment does not automatically renew validity. Renewal appends evidence and a new decision. Unknown validity permits record keeping and preparatory assessment, but cannot positively authorize an act requiring current authority.

Mongolia's Audit Law 3.1.3 defines auditor; 19.3.2–19.3.3 distinguish director/partner authority and minimum full-time CPA staffing; 19.4 adds firm-level requirements for the relevant full-IFRS audit category. These are **not** a requirement that every junior assistant, invited expert or ordinary platform user be a licensed audit partner. Capture the relevant personal/firm authorization and manual legal assessment for reserved roles. Article 20.4 prohibits the stated dual-employment/other-audit-firm contracting arrangements for CPAs employed by an audit organization; it is not a prohibition on handling multiple clients within the same firm. Do not model other-firm contractors as automatically eligible just because the software permits an invitation. The accepted D01 legal/ethics decision route handles actual exceptions and applicability.

## Classify the contribution before appointment

| Actual category | Applicable basis | D02 action and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Audit-team member, including specialized accounting/auditing expertise | ISA 220 (Revised), especially 12(d), 25–34 | Named team appointment, direction/supervision/review and applicable independence. An accounting consultation is not automatically ISA 620 merely because its author is called a specialist. |
| Auditor's internal expert in a non-accounting/auditing field | ISA 620.6(a), 7–13; ISA 220 (Revised) | Record actual employment/network context, competence/capability/objectivity, agreed work and evaluation; apply team obligations where the person is part of the team. |
| Auditor's external expert, e.g. lawyer or valuer | ISA 620.3, 6(a), 9–13, A14–A31 | Not an engagement-team member under ISA 220.12(d). Assess objectivity/interests and any applicable ethics/law; do not automatically impose every audit-team independence clause unchanged. Exact scope/files; return report; auditor evaluates adequacy. |
| Management's expert | ISA 500.8, A45–A59 in the 2020 handbook; ISA 620.2(b) | Evaluate evidence produced for management; record competence/capability/objectivity, understanding and appropriateness. Do not appoint them as an independent auditor's expert for the same self-reviewed work by changing a dropdown. |
| Component auditor | ISA 600 in accepted 2020 baseline: 19–20, 40–45, 50, A32–A41 | Group context, independence/competence, ability to be involved, instructions and communications; component contribution does not transfer the group partner's responsibility. D02 supplies appointment and access; substantive group-audit execution stays with its owning scope. |
| Internal audit function's existing work | ISA 610 (Revised 2013).15–25, 36 | Evaluate organizational objectivity, competence and systematic approach, proposed reliance, adequacy and reperformance. Client employee does not become an independent audit-team member. |
| Internal auditor providing direct assistance | ISA 610.26–35, 37 | Specific permissibility assessment, competence/objectivity, restricted tasks, written agreements with entity and individual, external direction/review and evidence checks. No significant audit judgments or prohibited self-review/higher-risk work. Record the local-rule conclusion for the actual context; standards do not override prohibition. No general statement that Mongolia forbids or universally allows every such arrangement is inferred. |
| EQR / EQR assistant | ISQM 2.17–23 | Outside engagement team, eligible, sufficient time and authorized appointment; separate review role and access. |

Category is determined by **who commissioned the work, the actual subject and intended use**, not by job title or portal permissions. If a contribution has more than one purpose, separate the work/evaluation records rather than grant the broadest role. A client's litigation lawyer answering a direct audit enquiry can also be an external-information response under ISA 501.9–12; it is not automatically an auditor-appointed ISA 620 expert. Record the actual relationship and route.

## Expert agreement and evaluation workflow

1. Senior records the audit need, relevant matter/assertion, actual contributor category and intended use. Identify responsible auditor and supervisor; retain category rationale. Where the underlying work involves a financial-reporting estimate, pass its selected IFRS/IAS framework, period and relevant method references to the specialist; D02 does not invent an accounting valuation method.
2. Record competence, practical ability/time/resources and objectivity inquiries; interests/relationships, management involvement and prior services are facts for evaluation. The printed 1.4.7 five-year employment statement is a source declaration, **not a universal ISA 620 prohibition**. Qualified disclosure opens an assessed matter; it is not silently false/failed or automatically cleared. Reuse D01's evidence/assessment route.
3. Agree nature/scope/objectives, respective responsibilities, source-data testing responsibility, methods/technical standards, limitations and permitted uses, deliverable format/date, communication contacts, confidentiality and working-paper access/retention. V1 records a written agreement for external sharing as a selected control; ISA 620.11 itself says writing when appropriate.
4. Senior authorizes the exact packet, recipient and secure channel; record any needed disclosure authority/NDA. Specialist acknowledges scope and returns a signed/attributable report or supporting evidence. An auditor may record a manually received opinion with sender, actual recipient, received date, original and checksum; never impersonate a specialist signature.
5. Responsible auditor evaluates findings/conclusions against other evidence, significant assumptions/methods and relevant source-data completeness/accuracy. Record procedures, inconsistencies, qualifications, conclusion adequate / further-work / unusable, reviewer and linked evidence versions. File receipt or specialist signature never equals audit acceptance.
6. Insufficient work returns for defined further work or additional audit procedures; unresolved evidence limitations flow to the existing audit-conclusion/reporting workflow. The expert cannot approve their own adequacy, resolve their own audit-review notes or authorize the audit opinion. The audit team's responsibility remains.

Form 1.4.7 contains performance/compliance/other-assurance placeholders. For this financial-statement-audit product, record not applicable with the selected service context; those printed lines do not authorize new services.
