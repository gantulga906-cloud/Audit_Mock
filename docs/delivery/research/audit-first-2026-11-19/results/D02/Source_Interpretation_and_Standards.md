## Applicable standards and limits

The accepted Mongolian compilation is ISA 2020 with integrated ISQM 1/2, ISA 220 (Revised) and conforming amendments. The 2020 English handbook contains both older material and later pronouncements at its back. Use **revised ISA 220**, not its superseded main-body predecessor. For cross-references affected by the quality amendments, the integrated quality-management text governs; “quality control” wording remaining in an earlier ISA copy is not a decision to retain ISQC 1.

ISA 220 supports sufficient resources, collective competence/time, direction/review and partner responsibility. ISA 230 supports attributable performance/review documentation. ISQM 1.31–32 supports supervision/resources; ISQM 2 addresses EQR eligibility. These do not mandate a universal hours threshold, fixed staff ratio, optimization algorithm, specific database layout, unlimited assignment authority or blanket independent approval of every administrative transfer. Multiple people, lead assignments and arithmetic are product requirements; non-self-approval on substantive work is the retained product control.

ISA 620 applies to auditor-used expertise outside accounting/auditing. Management's expert is evaluated under ISA 500.8; legal enquiry under ISA 501 is distinct; component work under ISA 600 and internal-audit work/direct assistance under ISA 610 have separate conditions. Distinct type, suitability, scope, evidence and evaluation are required. Narrowly scoped work remains possible; the software must not block all specialist use merely because an expert is not a CPA.

The local 2023 ethics code's 2018 international-code reference and D01's verified Audit Law are retained. Review current local authority manually with evidence for regulated roles; a CFA upload is not local statutory audit authority. ISA 610.26 expressly defers to legal restrictions; an engagement-specific permissibility assessment is part of the defined workflow. No blanket Mongolian direct-assistance prohibition was established by the retrieved Audit Law/ethics text. This does not turn silence into universal permission. Record actual service, employment/contract context and supported legal conclusion before direct assistance; existing-function work is a different branch.

**Accounting-standard applicability:** D02 measures staff minutes, availability and workflow states, not financial-statement recognition/measurement. No IAS/IFRS formula is applicable to these staffing ratios. A valuer/actuary's substantive assignment must carry the engagement's selected accounting framework and relevant method scope. D02's classification/agreement supports this; it does not implement an IFRS valuation, employee-benefit measurement or payroll engine.

## Contrary evidence checked and resolved

- Newer international ISA 600 and expert/ethics amendments exist. The IAASB's January 2026 expert amendment and 2026 handbook are recorded as **future/profile review triggers**, not silently imposed on the settled Mongolian baseline. The 2026 handbook identifies the later effective material separately. This is edition control, not an unresolved D02 baseline question.
- Small-firm scalability means job titles need not require separate people for every capacity. Retain flexible senior preparation, but prohibit approval of own substantive contribution under this product's chosen control. EQR's separate eligibility is not waived by small size.
- A person's available week may exceed total planned week while one day is overbooked. M35 evaluates daily conflicts and preserves signed total headroom; it never hides the daily excess by netting against another day.
- A professional certificate may not expire, but permission to practise or the person's standing may change. Separate evidence validity, current authority and task-specific competence rather than impose annual expiry on everything.
- Revocation cannot recall downloaded bytes. Portal tests cover future access; manual/provider records distinguish requested deletion from confirmed action. No impossible guarantee is promised.
