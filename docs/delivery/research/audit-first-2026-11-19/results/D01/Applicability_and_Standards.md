# D01 applicability and standards

**Research date:** 19 November 2026. Sources and retrieval limits: [Sources.csv](Sources.csv). Rule identifiers: [Blocker_Register.csv](Blocker_Register.csv). Read with the controlling [resolved D01 design](D01_Resolved_Design.md). Earlier open questions now have explicit dispositions; no professional sign-off is implied.

## Applicability record required before positive acceptance

Store one immutable assessment version per client, engagement/period and decision date. An enquiry may begin with incomplete facts. A missing mandatory fact produces an unresolved assessment, not an assumed exemption.

| Fact / decision | Required evidence | Decision owner | Unknown outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legal entity and jurisdiction | Registration document, legal name, identifiers and parent/related-entity information relevant to the rule | Acceptance preparer; reviewer confirms | Draft enquiry allowed; no definitive entity-based clearance |
| Reporting framework classification | Client facts; Accounting Law 4.1-4.4; applicable ministry classification or instrument | Practicing auditor; local adviser for ambiguity | No automatic SME/IPSAS or full-IFRS classification |
| Financial period and service | Exact period boundaries, service requested, intended users | Preparer and reviewer | Terms/positive acceptance incomplete |
| Audit standards edition | Mongolian ISA 2020 compilation with included quality updates; direct MonICPA confirmation, IFAC assessment and identified additional requirements | Methodology lead | Use the established baseline; clarify actual additional requirements where relevant |
| Ethical edition and entity classification | Local adoption evidence, relevant PIE/publicly traded or other classification and applicable relationships | Ethics reviewer | Do not default to a less demanding category |
| Firm eligibility | Permit scope/status as of decision, evidence issuer/date, required professional eligibility attestation | Authorized partner; manual registry/document check | Hold positive acceptance where eligibility cannot be established |
| Firm tenure and return history | Prior service years, service types, replacement basis and entity framework classification | Partner + local reviewer | No inferred tenure clearance |
| Individual tenure / relationships | Person-specific prior roles and relationships for the applicable ethical edition | Ethics reviewer | Affected person's clearance unresolved |
| Same-year services | Firm/provider, actual services, recipient and financial-year overlap | Partner + local reviewer | No article 9.5 clearance based on a generic service label |
| Contract validity | Actual agreement version, authorized signatories, signing mode/date and supporting evidence | Authorized partner; legal review when needed | Draft terms allowed; no activation |

Country, period and edition are separate fields. A legal entity's current classification does not rewrite its previous reporting periods. A later official publication does not retrospectively replace the adopted edition.

## Standards map: exact locator before implementation

These references support the versioned profile in the resolved design; 2026 material is not automatically local authority.

| Work | International source / locator | Local status in this research |

| Limits of legal-compliance work | ISA 250 (Revised), paragraph 4 and relevant requirements | Does not justify a universal legal compliance engine |

| Independence / services / relationships | IESBA Part 4A; selected Sections 400, 410, 420, 510, 521, 522, 524, 540 and 600 | Local 2023 code explicitly identifies the 2018 international reference; 2026 remains a comparison |

The 2026 handbook contains provisions with different effective dates. In particular, do not turn expert-related amendments or other future provisions into current mandatory controls merely because they appear in that volume. Confirm their individual effective and transitional provisions for the engagement.

## Local statutory findings

**Primary source:** current Mongolian legal text, Audit Law and Accounting Law, read on 19 November 2026. English explanations here are working translations; the Mongolian text controls. See the resolved decisions L01-L06 and Baseline_Decision.json.

| Article | D01 consequence to investigate / record | Important limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accounting Law 4.1-4.4 | Framework/category affects applicability | Not a name-, legal-form- or size-only classifier |
| Audit Law 4.1, 18.1.1, 19, 22 | Manual permit/eligibility evidence and status check | No automatic CPA/permit API; imported claims are unverified |
| Audit Law 7.1-7.4 | Independence and specified relationship/fee restrictions | Cross-referenced statutory definitions must be qualified |
| Audit Law 8.1-8.3 | Five consecutive years; replacement/three-year return provision; 8.1 exception for Accounting Law 4.1.2 and 4.1.3 entities | Count method and actual exemption must be documented; individual rotation is separate |
| Audit Law 9.5 | Same financial-year tax/accounting consultancy restriction | Do not restore the removed valuation wording from stale translations |
| Audit Law 13.1-13.2 | Paper/electronic contract and required commercial terms | A stored file hash does not establish signature authenticity |
| Audit Law 15.2.2 | Circumstances preventing audit require an appropriate refusal/other response | Withdrawal, notification and legal consequences require case-specific authority |

Article 7.2.6 contains a 5%-or-more investment condition and a 24-month period before contract. Retain the exact local source and fact evidence; do not infer the subject, denominator, related-person reach or calendar boundary from an English shorthand. Article 7.2.4 also needs a local interpretation of unsettled fees/other issues; it is not implemented as a universal prohibition on every nonzero invoice.

The 17 June 2022 amendment removed valuation wording from article 9.5; its commencement is tied to the relevant valuation legislation. The current consolidated Mongolian article confirms the removal. Historical engagements require the historical law in force; the amendment's signing date alone is not a historical effective-date rule.

## Edition-selection procedure

1. Preparer records engagement dates, jurisdiction, entity category and candidate instruments.
2. Reference the established Mongolian 2020 compilation and its MonICPA/IFAC confirmation. Methodology lead records any additional applicable requirements and their basis; individual ministerial instruments are not required to reopen the settled baseline.
3. Reviewer records a paragraph-level comparison where the source template uses a different edition.
4. Partner approves the applicability conclusion, evidence versions and unresolved limits.
5. Activation reads the approved version; a material fact/rule change opens a new assessment. Historical records retain the original basis.

The primary 2023 local ethics resolution/annex is identified; see Sources.csv. Direct MonICPA confirmation, corroborated by IFAC, establishes D01's Mongolian ISA 2020 compilation with integrated ISQM 1/2, revised ISA 220 and related conforming amendments. This closes the baseline adoption question; routine engagement applicability and additional firm requirements remain attributable auditor work. No calculation of accounting recognition or measurement is needed in D01; framework selection is the accounting-standard dependency.
