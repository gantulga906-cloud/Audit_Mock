## Authority and access

IAASB/IESBA original pronouncements and IFRS/IAS/IFRIC texts govern their respective international subjects; local primary law/adoption and approved firm policy determine actual applicability. National adoptions help compare wording but do not replace the selected international/local authority. Full official text or an authorized existing firm copy is preferred before spending on secondary material. Record access/licensing constraints before embedding copyrighted text in software.

## Important distinctions

1. **[high] International publication and future effective dates:** official issuer pages establish the stated editions/dates; they do not establish Mongolian adoption. See SRC-01/02/07/08/24/25/26.
2. **[med, reported] Local ISA/ethics adoption:** SRC-03 and SRC-04 report earlier ISA/ethics editions. They share underlying professional-body reporting and count as one evidence family, not two independent legal confirmations. Obtain MoF/MonICPA adoption evidence for each selected standard and period; quality-management adoption may differ from the ISA edition.
3. **[unverified] Mongolian primary provisions:** SRC-05/06 full text could not be fetched, including alternate translated routes. No article-level blocker, retention deadline, statutory tax rate or local prohibition is approved by this update. Best next check: authoritative consolidated Mongolian text plus dated amendments/approval instrument and a competent local reviewer.
4. **Method research still planned:** public standard summaries identify correct topic families. They do not validate ECL, lease, sampling, tax or other complete algorithms. Every M-card requires full applicable clauses, primary methods and independent examples.
