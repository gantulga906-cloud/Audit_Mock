## Work packages

<a id="r-d04-01"></a>

### R-D04-01 — Research risk taxonomy and assertion coverage

**Decision to resolve:** What distinctions are required between entity risks, FS risks, assertion risks and fraud risks?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D01-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Extract A04–A10/A12 steps, assertions, inquiry records, IT/service-organization context and prior-audit facts.
2. Read applicable ISA 315/240/300 editions; identify required evaluations and links without inventing a numerical risk scoring formula.
3. Specify many risks to many procedures, significant-risk reasoning and later risk reassessment.

**Required output:** Risk/response ontology, source/standard crosswalk and explicit decision records.

**Validation/contrary case:** New fraud concern, conflicting evidence, absent IT understanding and revised strategy cause appropriate targeted follow-up.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D04-02 — Separate control design, implementation and operating effectiveness

**Decision to resolve:** Which work justifies reliance, and what happens when it fails?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D04-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Map walkthrough/understanding evidence separately from design evaluation, implementation and period operating tests.
2. Research reliance decisions, indirect controls, IT dependencies, service auditor reports and internal-auditor use.
3. Define deficiency classification, communication, changed reliance and resulting substantive procedure changes.

**Required output:** Control-work contracts, reliance decision table and failure-to-programme handoff.

**Validation/contrary case:** A walkthrough cannot automatically pass operating effectiveness; failed controls cannot retain unchanged reliance without reason.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D04-03 — Research materiality policy and calculations

**Decision to resolve:** Which benchmarks, normalizations and percentages are firm judgments?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D01-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Reconcile source 1.3 and G02/G11 materiality with applicable ISA 320/450 texts.
2. Document benchmark selection, exceptional/negative bases, performance materiality, specific levels and clearly trivial threshold as distinct decisions.

**Required output:** Materiality policy decision memo, M03 specification and independently expected examples.

**Validation/contrary case:** No universal 5%, 60% or other source example becomes mandatory ISA policy; explicit zero trivial survives; changes preserve older approved bases.
