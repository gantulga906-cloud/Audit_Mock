## Work packages

<a id="r-d09-01"></a>

### R-D09-01 — Research misstatement classification and aggregation

**Decision to resolve:** How should signed errors, exposure, projection, omissions and prior-period effects coexist?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D07-03, R-D05-04 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Reconcile G05 with source A19–A24/A30 and ISA 450; distinguish factual, judgmental and projected misstatement.
2. Define monetary amount, debit/credit direction, gross exposure, approved netting, current/prior period and disclosure-only effects.
3. Specify cross-currency aggregation only through M22 and one underlying-event identity across analytics.

**Required output:** M33 finding/evaluation contract and independently expected aggregation examples.

**Validation/contrary case:** Opposite-sign errors cannot disappear through unjustified netting; unknown is not zero; projections and specifics contribute once.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D09-02 — Research correction verification and unresolved effects

**Decision to resolve:** What demonstrates an agreed adjustment actually reached the books?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D09-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Distinguish recommendation, management agreement, proposed journal, posted entry and independent verification.
2. Specify partial correction, reversals, changed ledgers, other-period posting and remaining amount under approved sign conventions.
3. Trace correction failure and reopened evidence into completion and reporting.

**Required output:** Correction lifecycle and source-linked partial/reversed/missing-posting fixtures.

**Validation/contrary case:** Agreement alone cannot clear a finding; partial posting leaves a current, reviewable residual.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

## Domain handoff and stop conditions

Deliver a source/standard paragraph crosswalk, AS-IS versus TO-BE delta, required/manual/specialist/HOLD disposition by subrequirement, approved input/action/result/review contract, adverse scenarios and unresolved decision records. The owner may size a ticket after its evidence and dependencies are known; no schedule or staffing commitment is inferred here.

Stop only the affected positive decision, calculation or release authorization when a mandatory source conflict, missing legal authority, unknown applicable edition, unsupported method or required independent review remains unresolved. Preserve drafts and unrelated approved work. Source-authorized manual judgment is supported; promised automatic calculations need an approved executable method or an explicit scope decision.
