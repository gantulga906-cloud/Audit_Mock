### R-D12-01 — Research later-fact dates and permitted alternatives

**Decision to resolve:** Which response is required at each report/issue/assembly stage?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D01-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Read original A27/A28 and current G07 full-redating behavior with applicable ISA 560.
2. Distinguish event date, discovery date, report date, issue date, management approval and final assembly date.
3. Research conditions for restricted procedures and dual dating, necessary management/governance action, recipient communication and new authorization.

**Required output:** Later-fact temporal decision table and authorized dual-dating method decision.

**Validation/contrary case:** Same fact discovered at different stages produces different documented work; unknown date cannot silently become today.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. ### R-D12-04 — Research custody, retrieval, inspection and recovery

**Decision to resolve:** Can the selected storage actually preserve and recover a reviewable audit file?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D12-03, R-D06-04 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Specify manifest/hash/readability, authorized transfer, inspector scope, chain of custody and receipt.
2. Research selected storage backups, restore consistency, encryption/key access and corruption detection; agree recovery objectives with owner.
3. Plan actual storage/replica/backups verification; separate local copied-database demonstration from production evidence.

**Required output:** Custody/recovery protocol, inspection grant cases and infrastructure handoff checklist.

**Validation/contrary case:** Recovered bytes/records match expected version; cross-client inspection and unauthorized rollback are denied; unrecovered copies are reported.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded.
