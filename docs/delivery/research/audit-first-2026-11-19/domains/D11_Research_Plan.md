## Work packages

<a id="r-d11-01"></a>

### R-D11-01 — Research opinion and report-structure decisions

**Decision to resolve:** Which report outcomes follow each source situation and combination?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D09-05, R-D10-03 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Read applicable full reporting standards and illustrative reports, source A26–A28 and current G06 decisions.
2. Separate known misstatement from inability to obtain evidence, materiality from pervasiveness, and going-concern/EOM/KAM/other-info roles.
3. Construct a decision table for multiple simultaneous causes; partner owns opinion, software checks allowed structure and current evidence.

**Required output:** Opinion compatibility matrix, rationale record and combined-cause scenario catalogue.

**Validation/contrary case:** EOM does not cure a modified opinion; KAM is not a substitute for a required qualification or material-uncertainty section.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D11-02 — Research exact report wording and applicability

**Decision to resolve:** Which template edition and local wording may be used for the selected report?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D11-01, R-D01-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Retrieve authorized templates and exact relevant standard illustrations; verify entity/framework/language and statutory additions.
2. Identify variable fields, mandatory sections, comparative information, addressees, signatures and dates.
3. Specify authorized practitioner edits, translation review, instance approval and immutable template/instance versions.

**Required output:** Report template applicability register and source-to-section contract.

**Validation/contrary case:** International illustration alone is not proof of local legally final wording; no automatic unsourced final prose.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D11-03 — Research authorization and issuance evidence

**Decision to resolve:** What exact state and bytes may be signed and released?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D11-02, R-D10-05 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Bind approved report to exact financial statements, notes, completion/quality decisions and report date.
2. Specify signing authority, stale inputs, local receipt/manual handoff, failed dispatch/retry and provider receipt semantics.
3. Keep external signature/dispatch adapters separate from local demonstration and preserve report successors.

**Required output:** Issuance state machine, byte/approval lineage and local/external boundary.

**Validation/contrary case:** Changed report cannot retain old signature authority; an attempted command is not an external delivery receipt.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

**Decision to resolve:** When are new wording, date, procedures or communications required?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D11-03, R-D12-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

## Domain handoff and stop conditions

Deliver a source/standard paragraph crosswalk, AS-IS versus TO-BE delta, required/manual/specialist/HOLD disposition by subrequirement, approved input/action/result/review contract, adverse scenarios and unresolved decision records. The owner may size a ticket after its evidence and dependencies are known; no schedule or staffing commitment is inferred here.

Stop only the affected positive decision, calculation or release authorization when a mandatory source conflict, missing legal authority, unknown applicable edition, unsupported method or required independent review remains unresolved. Preserve drafts and unrelated approved work. Source-authorized manual judgment is supported; promised automatic calculations need an approved executable method or an explicit scope decision.
