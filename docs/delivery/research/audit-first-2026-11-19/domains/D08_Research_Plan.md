### R-D08-01 — Reconcile all 22 source fieldwork variants

**Decision to resolve:** What exact procedures, assertions and exceptions belong to each original variant?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D03-01, R-D05-03 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Open every variant's exact form/cell/page locators from fieldwork_variants and G10, including visible figures and instructions.
2. Decompose each procedure into input, actual action, judgment/calculation, evidence, conclusion, review and findings/report consequences.
3. Join to area-specific research cards; cross-cutting lease/FX/estimate/group work augments original areas without inventing replacement variant IDs.

**Required output:** 22 individually scoped fieldwork research cards and source procedure inventory.

**Validation/contrary case:** All V01–V22 remain distinct, including other receivables, deferred income, COS and other-income variants; no consolidation into nine vague groups.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.
