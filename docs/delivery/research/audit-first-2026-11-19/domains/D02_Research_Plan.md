## Work packages

<a id="r-d02-01"></a>

### R-D02-01 — Define appointments and many-person allocation

**Decision to resolve:** Who may allocate, transfer, prepare and review each item across engagements?

**Entry/dependencies:** Source baseline accessible; this task can begin without waiting for unrelated domain research. Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Read source role/actions and G09 grants; model engagement membership separately from work-item appointments.
2. Map lead/secondary preparers, reviewer, manager and partner capacities, temporary substitutions and effective dates.
3. Define transfer of open work, retained authorship, outstanding notes and conflicts after reassignment.

**Required output:** Assignment state model, cardinalities, action table and source-linked transfer scenarios.

**Validation/contrary case:** Three or more people, concurrent engagements and transfer during review preserve authorship and prohibit self-approval.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D02-02 — Research capacity and scenario variance

**Decision to resolve:** What does capacity mean, and what evidence supports a staffing conflict?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D02-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Specify available hours, working calendars, leave, time zones, planned allocation and actual hours; define period and denominator.
2. Research simple capacity/variance measures against current source requirements; do not invent utilization thresholds or quality scores.
3. Model mutually exclusive scenarios, shared staff, overbooking, changed budgets and unsubmitted actuals.

**Required output:** Approved M35 definitions, staffing input contract and scenario comparison fixture.

**Validation/contrary case:** Part-time staff, holiday overlap, zero availability, missing timesheets and changed assignments give explainable results.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D02-03 — Specify credential evidence and expiry

**Decision to resolve:** What can be asserted from an uploaded qualification rather than a live issuer check?

**Entry/dependencies:** Source baseline accessible; this task can begin without waiting for unrelated domain research. Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Distinguish identity, credential type/issuer/ID, expiry, scope of practice and competence evidence.
2. Define self-reported / file-reviewed / externally verified as different evidence states, with evaluator and date.
3. Research whether specific regulated work needs current local authorization; leave external checks with designated integration owners.

**Required output:** Credential schema and expiry/review policy linked to appointment decisions.

**Validation/contrary case:** An uploaded CFA/CPA image never becomes an externally verified credential automatically; expired authority triggers the applicable work restriction.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. ### R-D02-04 — Research specialist appointment and confidentiality

**Decision to resolve:** What evidence may an expert receive, and how is the expert's work evaluated?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D01-01, R-D02-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Compare auditor's expert, management's expert, component auditor and internal auditor under applicable standards; do not conflate their responsibilities.
2. Specify competence/objectivity/authority checks, agreed scope, limitations and audit team's evaluation of adequacy.
3. Define selected-file or engagement-selected-set grants, expiry/revocation/download, opinion return and secure manual handoff.

**Required output:** Specialist agreement/evaluation checklist, grant contract and manual/portal boundary.

**Validation/contrary case:** Lawyer can return a legal opinion for permitted files; cannot clear audit opinion or access other clients; direct URLs fail after revocation.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

## Domain handoff and stop conditions

Deliver a source/standard paragraph crosswalk, AS-IS versus TO-BE delta, required/manual/specialist/HOLD disposition by subrequirement, approved input/action/result/review contract, adverse scenarios and unresolved decision records. The owner may size a ticket after its evidence and dependencies are known; no schedule or staffing commitment is inferred here.

Stop only the affected positive decision, calculation or release authorization when a mandatory source conflict, missing legal authority, unknown applicable edition, unsupported method or required independent review remains unresolved. Preserve drafts and unrelated approved work. Source-authorized manual judgment is supported; promised automatic calculations need an approved executable method or an explicit scope decision.
