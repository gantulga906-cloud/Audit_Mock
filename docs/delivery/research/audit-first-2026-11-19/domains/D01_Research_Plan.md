## Work packages

<a id="r-d01-01"></a>

### R-D01-01 — Determine standards and entity applicability

**Decision to resolve:** Which framework, audit edition, ethical edition and entity classifications govern this engagement?

**Entry/dependencies:** Source baseline accessible; this task can begin without waiting for unrelated domain research. Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Inspect original A01–A03, client/terms commands, source decisions and guidebooks; distinguish engagement acceptance from contract signing and activation.
2. Retrieve official standard texts and adoption instruments for the selected period; obtain entity facts needed for listed/PIE/public-sector/SME classification.
3. Record international effective rule, local adoption evidence and firm policy separately. Escalate conflicts rather than selecting the newest PDF.

**Required output:** Applicability decision table, evidence checklist, unresolved adoption questions and affected source IDs.

**Validation/contrary case:** Two engagements with different periods retain different editions; unknown classification cannot silently choose a less demanding rule.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D01-02 — Research absolute blockers and review-only conditions

**Decision to resolve:** Which restrictions are nonwaivable, and which require investigation or safeguards?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D01-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Extract candidate conditions from ISA 210, applicable independence sections and verified local licensing/prohibition provisions.
2. For each condition write facts required, source paragraph/article, effective dates, affected action and permitted resolution authority.
3. Classify block / requires professional review / informational; challenge with contrary exceptions, continuance, withdrawal and missing evidence.

**Required output:** Versioned blocker register and action-specific acceptance decision table; local-law rows remain unverified until primary text and authorized interpretation exist.

**Validation/contrary case:** Prohibited positive acceptance fails; unresolved evidence allows drafting but cannot support positive acceptance; changed evidence renews the decision.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.
