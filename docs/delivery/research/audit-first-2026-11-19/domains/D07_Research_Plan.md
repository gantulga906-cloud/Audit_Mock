## Work packages

<a id="r-d07-01"></a>

### R-D07-01 — Research method support and sampling purpose

**Decision to resolve:** Which sampling designs are required by the approved source/offering?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D04-03, R-D05-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Read native form 3.3.1, A11–A13, current G04 implementation and source minimum AUTO-047.
2. Compare tests of controls, tests of details, specific-item testing, 100% testing, statistical and nonstatistical sampling.
3. List required/conditional/manual/unsupported methods including stratification and MUS; source optionality cannot silently delete a required calculation.

**Required output:** Sampling support matrix and method-specific input/output schemas.

**Validation/contrary case:** A random selector alone cannot claim a statistical evaluation; targeted items cannot be projected to untested residuals without a valid design.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D07-02 — Research sample size and selection implementation

**Decision to resolve:** What assumptions justify size, confidence and selection probabilities?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D07-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. For each selected design retrieve applicable audit requirements plus original statistical/methodological references, not generic survey formulas.
2. Specify unit, population frame, expected error/deviation, tolerable amount/rate, sampling risk, strata and treatment of negatives/zeros.
3. Specify M04 reproducibility/unbiased index mapping, M05 numeric algorithm or documented human size, and independent reference cases.

**Required output:** Approved selection/size technical notes with explicit scope, prerequisites and rejection cases.

**Validation/contrary case:** Duplicate units, changed population, seed/version replay, impossible size and unsupported signs fail transparently; no invented confidence bound.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D07-03 — Research projection and evaluation

**Decision to resolve:** How do tested specifics, sampled errors and projected residuals reach findings once?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D07-02 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Specify signed error convention, ratio/difference/MUS estimators where selected, strata, anomaly evidence and sampling-risk allowance.
2. Distinguish total projected estimate from incremental unsampled component and factual error already included.
3. Define unavailable tests, replacements, expansion, inability to evaluate and new design/version rules.

**Required output:** M06/M33 handoff contract, independently computed projections and anti-duplication fixtures.

**Validation/contrary case:** Existing 1,000/200/10 illustration produces a total 50 only under its approved assumptions; no duplicate factual 10; zero denominator is non-evaluable.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded. Use [findings template](../templates/Research_Findings_Template.md). Engineering/practitioner acceptance remains a later separate gate.

### R-D07-04 — Research substantive analytics and journal investigation

**Decision to resolve:** What can a rule flag, and what requires investigation?

**Entry/dependencies:** R-D04-01, R-D05-01 Dependencies gate affected conclusions/tickets, not all independent reading or drafting.

1. Research ISA 520 expectations, data reliability, precision and acceptable differences; research applicable fraud/journal responsibilities.
2. Compare variance/ratio/trend methods and journal signatures with documented population, thresholds and false-positive cases.
3. Specify evidence of performed investigation, explanations, corroboration, revised risk and findings rather than automatic fraud conclusions.

**Required output:** M07/M08 method notes, native workpaper contract and source-linked investigation examples.

**Validation/contrary case:** Perfect rule coverage does not prove fraud absence; whole-journal flags share one exposure identity; inconsistent denominators do not become zero.

**Close research only when:** full-source/standard evidence, conflicting interpretation, supported boundary, actual owner decision and implementation-ready scenarios are recorded.
